# Scope, language decisions and verification

This cumulative release translates OLP-0004–OLP-0019: complete Sets and Relations, fourteen substantive sections and two drivers. It includes source examples, exercises, proofs, figures and novice/math/computer-science branches. Conditional later-chapter references use the original's alternative. All 722 units remain the full-edition goal, including explicit treatment of alternative wrappers and formal-only material in a coherent reader. This release is not full completion.

## Checks and limitations

The sixteen units have 190 aligned linguistic blocks with exact source/target byte ranges, hashes and actual consulted passage IDs. Two batches verify 844 math spans, identifiers/references/assets/citations, environments, braces and OLP tokens. Linguistic text inside math is translated; local formula order can follow Gujarati syntax while multiplicity and meaning remain unchanged. Gujarati files are NFC without replacement characters or Devanagari contamination.

All source blocks were compared during drafting. Twenty-two reverse-paraphrase samples, thirteen new in Relations, check quantifier scope, membership/inclusion, Russell's contradiction, antisymmetry/asymmetry, proof direction, graph incidence, predecessor uniqueness and positive-length closure. This is same-agent review, not independent human or native fluency certification.

All nineteen LuaLaTeX pages were visually inspected. One English part heading was corrected; the other seventeen pages stayed pixel-identical and the two changed pages were reinspected. Final guarded passes are byte-identical. No missing glyphs, overfull boxes or unresolved references appeared. The shell-escape-disabled notice is expected.

PDF visual correctness does not imply perfect extraction: PyMuPDF often joins Gujarati words and installed Poppler sometimes displaces vowel signs. Offline HTML supplies Unicode and native MathML without JavaScript/external runtime dependencies: 842 MathML nodes, six images with Gujarati alt text, no broken anchors, and the exact 25,036-character Gujarati sequence of its prepared body. Graph/tree sections were visually inspected in the browser.

## Source fidelity

Editable identities/macros are preserved; proper-name diacritics are transliterated with the names. Adapters expand article/plural tokens into Gujarati inflections. Math notation follows frozen defaults. Set SVGs reproduce original TikZ paths/colors. New graph/tree SVGs preserve validated vertex/edge incidence with deterministic layout, including self-loop, isolated vertex and upward-growing tree.

Separate notes flag inherited issues without changing formulas: I names the natural-number diagonal without a separate assignment; the branch definition uses undefined X where the tree's base set is A; the subtree example does not explicitly exclude the empty subset despite the rooted-tree definition. R+ has different local definitions in Orders and Operations; both remain. Bibliographic titles/names retain their source language. Early set-existence wording remains alongside the later Russell qualification.
