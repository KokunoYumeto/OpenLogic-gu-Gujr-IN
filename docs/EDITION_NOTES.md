# Scope, language decisions, and verification

The cumulative working edition translates OLP-0004–OLP-0040: complete Sets, Relations, Functions, and Size of Sets. These 37 units comprise thirty-three substantive sections and four chapter drivers. Source examples, exercises, proofs, figures, captions, footnotes, and novice, mathematical, computer-science, and alternative set-theory branches are included. The full-edition goal remains all 722 units, including coherent treatment of alternative wrappers and formal-only material.

## Actual Gujarati canon

Thirteen acquired-source records and fifty-six exact page or paragraph passages are indexed with hashes, locators, inspection notes, evidence roles, and short attestations. Forty-one passages are visually inspected page renders; fifteen are exact byte slices from local extracts. The eight CRLF-derived locators identified by GU-PROV-001 separately bind the acquired source, raw extract, and LF-normalized locator stream. Their declared normalization replaces each raw `0D 0A` pair with `0A` and performs no decoding, re-encoding, Unicode normalization, or other transformation.

GSSTB mathematics pages directly support set operations, ordered pairs, relations, function kinds, inverses, and composition. Gujarati Vishwakosh prose supplies concept-specific usage for sets, predicates, countability, equinumerosity, infinite size, and induction. Recovered Samagra Shiksha pages contribute only their verified instruction and numeracy register; historical consultation or QA was not inherited. Mathematical simplifications and differing natural-number or countability conventions in language witnesses never override the frozen English source.

Set remains ગણ and algebraic group remains સમૂહ. Adopted choices include ઘટક, ઉપગણ, ઘાતગણ, યોગગણ, છેદગણ, સંબંધ, પ્રદેશ, સહપ્રદેશ, વિસ્તાર, વિધેય, એક-એક, વ્યાપ્ત, પ્રતિવિધેય, સંયોજન, ગણનીય, અગણનીય, સામ્ય ગણો, ગણસામ્ય, and ગણાંક. Specialized labels without direct concept-specific attestation are marked provisional in the 68-entry term ledger. In particular, OpenLogic's local definition of ગણનીય includes finite and empty sets even though one canon witness uses the word only for countably infinite sets.

Third-party canon originals and full extracts remain local research evidence and are not redistributed. Exact reuse, duplicate, and non-import decisions for recovered material appear in `provenance/LAPTOP_CANON_RECONCILIATION.json`.

## Checks and present release status

The 37 units have 464 aligned linguistic blocks with exact source and target byte ranges, hashes, and actually consulted passage IDs. Four batch receipts verify 2,397 source math spans together with identifiers, references, assets, citations, conditionals, environments, balanced braces, OLP tokens, NFC, and script hygiene. Formula differences are accepted only when keyed to a disclosed source correction. Linguistic material inside math is translated, and local formula order may follow Gujarati grammar while mathematical meaning and multiplicity remain controlled.

All source blocks were compared during drafting. Ninety-one reverse-paraphrase samples check definitions, quantifier scope, proof direction, edge cases, relation properties, function restrictions and inverses, diagonal arguments, reductions, countability constructions, cardinal comparison, and Schröder–Bernstein. This is same-agent semantic review, not independent native-speaker certification.

The cumulative offline reader contains 2,370 MathML nodes, eleven described SVG diagrams, 128 unique IDs, three footnotes, and seventeen visible source-correction boxes. Automated QA found no broken internal links and preserved the exact Gujarati character sequences of the prepared body and footnotes. Live browser inspection covered the title and table of contents, representative tables and diagrams, every Size correction region, alternative sections, editorial notes, bibliography, and footnotes.

The 37-unit PDF remains a release candidate until the machine-wide TeX slot is available. Publication requires three guarded passes with matching final hashes followed by all-page rendered visual inspection. The latest publicly verified release therefore remains Sets and Relations v0.2.0 at 16/722 units.

## Disclosed source corrections

Seventeen keyed notes keep each deliberate departure from the frozen English source reviewable. OLFUN-001–OLFUN-005 repair the empty-domain left-inverse claim, the square-root zero boundary, an `n`/`x` typo, the graph relation type, and the restriction analogy. OLSIZ-001–OLSIZ-012 restore a missing table value, a malformed cofinite sentence, a duplicated pairing family, two free-index characteristic sequences, a finite object in an infinite-sequence codomain, two undefined-function branches, a quantified domain, reversed string indices, a repeated bit flip, malformed three-argument conditional braces, and a duplicate fully qualified exercise label. Each correction records the frozen source hash, target location, rationale, exact mathematical change where applicable, and semantic and render status.

Uncorrected inherited issues remain separate editorial notes. These include the natural-number diagonal named without a separate assignment, an undefined `X` where a tree base set is `A`, a subtree example that does not explicitly exclude the empty subset, and different local meanings of `R+` in Orders and Operations. Bibliographic titles and names retain their source language.

## Source fidelity and rendering

Editable identities, macro names, labels, citations, links, hierarchy, and attribution are preserved except for the single disclosed duplicate-label repair. Adapters expand article and plural tokens into Gujarati inflections. Set diagrams reproduce the original TikZ paths and colors; function diagrams preserve the source mapping incidence; graph and tree diagrams preserve validated vertices, edges, loops, isolation, and growth direction.

PDF extraction is not used as a proxy for visual correctness because Gujarati shaping can be misreported by extraction tools. The offline reader supplies Unicode Gujarati and native MathML without an external JavaScript runtime. Provisional terminology, source-correction disclosures, and the incomplete 37/722 coverage remain visible to readers.
