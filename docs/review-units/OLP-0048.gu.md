number, like $\sqrt{2}$, has a nice decimal expansion, beginning:

	1.41421356237\ldots

the decimal point (here, just $1$). We will also need a further tweak

construction of the real numbers, in the manner of Stevin, within set
theory.

		f(n)&\text{otherwise}
	\end{cases}\\

\]
